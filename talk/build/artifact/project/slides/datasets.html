[1 min] SAY: To answer that question, we used free, public data. We love public data, because anyone can check our work. A digital elevation model tells us how high the land is. Landsat satellites have photographed this valley since 1984. Sentinel-1 is a radar satellite. It sees through cloud, so it keeps working in the rainy season, when floods happen. And a land-cover map tells us what grows where. Keep one idea in your head: every public dataset is built from other public datasets. The 38-year water map is made from Landsat photos. Our height-above-stream map is made from the elevation model. Public data has a family tree. NEXT: first, how do we score a model?
