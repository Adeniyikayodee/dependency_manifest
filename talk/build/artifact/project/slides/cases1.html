[1 min] SAY: So how common is this? Very. Two researchers at Princeton went looking for exactly this kind of mistake in published science. They found leakage in 329 papers, across 17 different fields. Medicine, politics, biology, and more. These are careful, clever people, and their papers passed peer review. It’s an easy trap, because everything runs and the score looks great. NEXT: and it shows up in big, well-funded work on public data too.
