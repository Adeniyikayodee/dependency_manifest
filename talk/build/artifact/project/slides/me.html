[45 sec] SAY: So who am I, and how did I end up in Katcha? I’m Kayode. I used to lead research at GRMI, the Geohazards Risk Mapping Initiative, a youth-led non-profit in Abuja. We make flood maps one village at a time, because national maps are too coarse to tell you which street or which farm floods. That photo is me doing flood fieldwork in a rice field in Katcha. Today I’m a PhD researcher at Imperial, on an AI for Science project funded by Schmidt Sciences. My job, then and now: turn free public data into maps people can act on. NEXT: my path, in one line.
