[1 min] SAY: Here’s the project. There’s a rice called FARO 67. It can sit under water for two weeks and still grow back. It’s bred at a research institute just down the road from Katcha. We had 200 kilos of it, enough for 20 farmers. That’s about four football pitches, in a place with thousands of hectares of rice. So everything came down to one question: which fields flood first? That’s a prediction problem. And we used free, public data to answer it. NEXT: here’s the data we used.
