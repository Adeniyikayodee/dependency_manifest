[1 min] [Slow down. This is the why.] SAY: Before any code, here’s why we care about this. Floods are the most common disaster on the planet. About one person in four, 1.81 billion people, lives where a big flood, the kind that comes once in a hundred years, would put their home under water. And between 2000 and 2019, floods were 44 per cent of all disasters recorded. More than storms, more than earthquakes. A wrong flood map sends help to the wrong place.
