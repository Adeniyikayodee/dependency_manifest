[1 min] SAY: Here are some of those projects, one number each. In Ikorodu in 2021, 55 volunteers from 15 countries mapped 11,000 buildings in a single day. In Borno in 2022, we checked the flood risk of camps where about 35,000 displaced people live. In 2023 we helped over 420 women farmers in 74 communities get ready for flood season. In 2024, with University of Jos YouthMappers, we mapped 9,721 buildings in Jos North. In 2025, after the Mokwa flood, volunteers mapped more than 2,000 homes in six days to guide relief teams. And in 2026, Katcha: twenty farmers with flood-tolerant seed. That’s tonight’s story. NEXT: the people we work with.
